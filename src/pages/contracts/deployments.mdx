# Deployments

Below are the addresses of the protocol-wide MakinaX contracts. Per-Safe modules are deployed by the [`ModuleFactory`](/contracts/factory/contract.ModuleFactory) and are not listed here.

The infrastructure contracts are deployed deterministically and share the same addresses on every chain. The `AccessManager` governing them differs per chain: on Ethereum and Base it is the Makina Core `AccessManager`, while Optimism has a dedicated instance.
